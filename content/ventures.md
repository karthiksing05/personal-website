> All models are wrong, but some are useful. *— George E. P. Box, British statistician*

Most of my work lives in research labs, but some of my favorite projects have come from the moments where an idea gets a deadline, a team, and someone on the other side who needs it to actually work. It is in these moments, where a result has to hold up in front of judges and users rather than reviewers, that I have learned the most about which of my ideas are worth building. This page collects those efforts - hackathon builds, research sprints, and the products that came out of them - each with the month it was built, what came of it, and a link to the full story (the longer writeups live right here on the site as their own pages!).

I have recently taken to venture-capitalism as a late stage career and am actively interested in entrepreneurial ventures that promote meaningful and deliberate advances in humanity - would love to reach out and connect with those interested!
