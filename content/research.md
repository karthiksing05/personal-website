> The work is its own reward. *— Sir Arthur Conan Doyle, British writer and physician*

This page collects the research I have been lucky enough to take part in, from my first independent paper to the labs I am working with today. Most of it orbits the same question: how can we build machines that learn the way *people* do, incrementally, hierarchically, and with structure we can actually read? Each card shows when the work ran (ongoing efforts come first, and are marked as running to the present), the lab it lived in, what came out of it, and links to the code and papers behind it - please reach out if any of it sounds interesting!
